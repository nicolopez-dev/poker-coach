♠ Poker Coach

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Poker Coach teaches six-handed no-limit hold'em and helps you set up the chips for a home game. It keeps what it needs to remember your progress, and nothing else. There is no advertising, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is NiLo S.L., Camí de la Reineta 11, 08017 Barcelona, Spain. For anything in this policy, write to contact@pokercoach.app.

What is stored, and why

Everything below is held against your account so the app can show you the same progress on your next launch. The list is exhaustive — it is the same set of tables the in-app export returns, and there is no other store.

What Why
Your email address, and a password that is stored only as a hash To sign you in, confirm the address, and let you reset a forgotten password
A Google account identifier, if you sign in with Google To recognise you on the next sign-in. We receive your email address and an account id; we never receive your Google password
A display name and a chosen avatar To name you at the table. Neither has to be your real name
Hearts, streak, longest streak, the times they were last settled, and your device's time-zone offset Hearts refill on a clock and a streak turns over at your local midnight, so both need a time zone. The offset is a number of minutes — it is not a location
Every answer you give — the lesson, the question, the option you chose, whether it was right, and when Accuracy, XP and the week chart are worked out from this rather than stored as totals, so the numbers cannot drift from what you actually did
Every lesson you finish, with its score and the time To know where you are on the path and what to open next
Your chip case and your games — chip colours and values, player counts, entry bets, and the seat names, end counts and balances you enter To set up the next game without re-entering it

Seat names are about other people

When you record a home game you can type a name for each seat. Those are other people's names, entered by you, and they are stored with your games. Use first names or nicknames — the app never needs anyone's full identity, and nobody but you can read them.

What is not collected

  • No analytics and no tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service. Nothing follows you between apps or across the web.
  • No location. No GPS, no IP-based location lookup.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera. None are requested.
  • No payment details. There is nothing to buy.
  • No real money and no gambling. Stakes in the app are abstract units. No wagering of any kind takes place and there is nothing to cash out.
  • These web pages collect nothing. They load no fonts, scripts, cookies or third-party resources of any kind. Your host's ordinary server logs are the only record that you visited.

Who else processes it

These are the only third parties involved, and each is doing a job the app needs:

Who What they do
Supabase Hosts the database and handles sign-in. All of the data above lives there, in the European Union
Resend Delivers account email — confirmation and password reset. They process your address to send those messages, and send nothing else
Google Only if you choose "Continue with Google", and only to sign you in

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. It is disclosed otherwise only if the law requires it.

What stays on your phone

Your sign-in token is kept in the operating system's secure storage. A copy of your hearts, streak and progress is cached so the app can draw something on a cold start before the server answers, and lessons you finish without a connection wait in a queue on the device until it comes back. All of it is erased when you log out or delete your account, and none of it is readable by other apps.

How long it is kept

Until you delete your account. There is no separate retention period and no archive: the deletion described below removes the rows themselves rather than flagging them as hidden. Backups taken before a deletion roll off on their own schedule.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU, the GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Two of them are built into the app so you do not have to ask anyone:

Take a copy — You → Export my data

Writes every one of the tables above to a JSON file and hands it to your share sheet. It is indented so a person can read it, not only a machine.

Delete everything — You → Delete account

Removes the account and every row attached to it, immediately, with no recovery. See the deletion page for exactly what goes.

For anything else — rectification, restriction, objection, or a complaint — write to contact@pokercoach.app. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Our lawful bases

  • Performing our contract with you for your account, your progress and your saved games: without them there is no app to provide.
  • Legitimate interests for keeping the service secure and working — rate limits, and the checks that stop one account reading another's data.

Children

Poker Coach is not intended for children under 18, and accounts should not be created for them. It teaches a card game played for stakes, and the age rating on the store listing reflects that. If you believe a child has created an account, write to contact@pokercoach.app and it will be deleted.

Where your data is handled

Data is stored in the European Union. Where a processor moves personal data outside the UK or the EEA, it does so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and the app will say so before the change takes effect.
